Season square weighting

Twelve real 2026 seasons, replayed from recorded BWF responses. The top strip is today’s uniform rendering; the bottom scales each square so its area is proportional to the weight of the tournament, inside a fixed 52px slot — every event keeps the same footprint, lighter ones just leave more air around them.

BWF requires Top Committed Players (top 15 singles, top 10 doubles) to enter the World Tour Finals if qualified, every Super 1000, every Super 750, and two of the nine Super 500s. That makes Super 750 the line above which entry is compulsory — 10 of the 42 events on the 2026 calendar — which is what the default scheme uses as its full-size tier.
